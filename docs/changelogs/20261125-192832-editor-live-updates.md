# The open editor shows changes from another app without a reload

- While a draft is open and you are not typing, the editor checks every 4 seconds for changes from another app, and shows them as they arrive.
- Posts that changed are highlighted for a moment, and a line says "Updated just now".
- The checks stop when the page is hidden, slow down after 2 minutes idle, and pause after 10 minutes until you click Resume or act.

Area: Drafts
Link: https://github.com/Shaked/x-thread-draft-tool/issues/363
