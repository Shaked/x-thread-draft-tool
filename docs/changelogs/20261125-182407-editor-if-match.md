# The editor no longer overwrites changes from another app

- If another app changes a draft while you are editing it, the editor stops saving and shows a banner.
- The banner lets you take the other app's version, with your text kept to copy, or keep your version on top.
- A draft you did not edit is not saved again, even when you close the page.
